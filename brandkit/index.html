Four directions, one brand core

Every board uses the same brand: Montserrat, brand accent #E87722, ink #171717, the client logo and the client’s own photography. What changes is the expression: surface, type treatment, corners, rhythm, image grade and motion. Cinematic and Engineered are the primary pair; Editorial and Kinetic are the backups.

Cinematic home, Engineered home, Editorial home, Kinetic home

Dark and cinematic. The accent is the light in the frame: actions, key figures, the active state. All-sharp corners, with a notch cut from primary actions.
